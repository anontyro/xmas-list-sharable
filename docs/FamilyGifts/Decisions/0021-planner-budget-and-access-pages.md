# 0021 · Budgets and notes have their own page, and the recovery link is shown once because only a hash is stored

**Status:** Agreed · **Work items:** FG-506 · **Spec:** `Docs/FamilyGifts/Specs/2026-11-17-FG-002-sticker-book-design.md` D72, D73, D74, D75

The planner is read on one page, edited on another, and saving access is a page instead of a dialog. Money rules (decision 0011), exports and the recovery rules (decision 0015) did not change.

- **Planner page (D72).** Mint hero "My planner" with "Christmas 2026 · only you can see this", and a row of period links when the guest has more than one. One white card per recipient: a ribbon with the name (tone from `tintFor`) and "Budget £X" when set, then rows with a checkbox (`Select <gift>`), the title, the status pill, the price (muted for Interested), "paid £X" and "Not in totals" or "Removed by the owner" where they apply. Each row keeps its claim buttons (Release and Mark purchased still work here) and a dashed note box with "Edit note", or "Add note" when there is none. A "Shopping list" card shows "N of M selected", Select all, Clear, "Include my private notes", Copy list, Markdown, CSV and "Exports never include your recovery link or other people's plans." The buttons' accessible names stay "Download Markdown" and "Download CSV". The yellow total card sits in the sidebar (first on phones).
  Per-recipient totals are gone; only the recipient's budget shows. `PlannerView` now also carries every person budget for the period, so a budget for someone with no gifts yet is not lost.
- **Budget card and meter (D73).** "Total budget" with the amount, or "No budget yet" and "Set a budget to see how much is left." A meter with spent (green) and planned-but-not-spent (red) as shares of the budget, never over 100% (`meterSegments`, pure), labelled in words ("£24.99 spent and £129.99 more planned out of £250.00"). Tiles Planned, Spent and Left ("£X over" when over budget), and a note for what is not counted: interested gifts with their total, and unpriced items. Every figure is `computePlan`'s; the card adds nothing up except for the words.
- **Budget and notes (D74).** `/planner/budget`, for a guest only (everyone else sees the same "pick a name first" page as `/planner`; an owner has no planner). "Your budget": total, occasion, a budget for each person who has gifts or a budget already, "+ Add a person" for the rest, and a note when the people add up to the total (or to more than it). With `?gift=<itemId>` and only when that gift is in the guest's own planner for the period (a malformed or foreign id is ignored), a "Gift details" card adds what was paid (not for Interested gifts), the private note and "Count this gift in my totals". "Save changes" runs `saveBudgets` then `setEntryDetails`, shows the first error and reports nothing else as saved, and returns to the planner; Cancel just goes back. Switching the occasion reloads the page, so unsaved edits are dropped.
- **Save your access (D75).** `/access`, for a guest only (others go to `/welcome?next=/access`). Status pill from `recoveryLinkStatus`: "No link yet", "Active · made today" or "Active · made 3 Nov" (London dates). "Make a link" / "Make a new link" and "Turn link off". The link itself is shown only in the response to making it: it lives in the page's state, in a read-only field with a large Copy link button, with "Copy it now: for your safety we can't show it again." After a reload or leaving, the page shows the status and says it cannot show the link again. A "How it works" card, the warning that anyone with the link can open the planner, and "You're Sam on this browser". The account menu's "Save access (recovery link)" is a link here; the dialog is gone.
  **This differs from the mockup**, which shows the link on every visit: only a hash of the token is stored (decision 0015), so it cannot be shown again, and a new link is the way to get one.

## Why

A planner has a lot to read and a few things to change, so the page that reads is kept free of forms, and each form has one job and one Save. Private notes and the amount paid are one tap from the gift they belong to. The access link is as strong as a password, so it is shown once, kept only in page state, and a replaced or turned-off link fails with the same message as any bad link.

## Consequences

- Making a new link while one is active replaces it without asking (as the dialog did); devices already signed in stay signed in.
- Reaching a person's budget with no gifts needs "+ Add a person", then Save changes.
- A gift's note or price can only be edited from its own "Edit note" or "Add note" button, not inline on the planner.
- The page cannot tell a guest the link they made earlier; someone who did not save it has to make a new one.
